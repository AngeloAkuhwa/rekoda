# Rekoda — SEO & Content Plan

**Goal:** the marketing site is warming in the index while the product is
built, so Chat launches to organic demand rather than a cold domain.

## Technical SEO (ships with M1)

SSR via Next.js + Cloudflare CDN (Core Web Vitals on Nigerian mobile) ·
schema.org on every page (`SoftwareApplication`, `FAQPage`, `Article`,
`BreadcrumbList`) · per-page OG images · sitemap/robots discipline (index
marketing + guides; never documents, dashboards, webhooks) · Plausible
analytics (cookieless) · UTM discipline on every `wa.me` link so page →
WhatsApp conversion is measurable.

## Keyword clusters (validated Nigerian search demand)

1. **Tool-seeking:** invoice app for small business Nigeria · receipt maker
   Nigeria · bookkeeping app for small business · how to send invoice on
   WhatsApp · invoice template Nigeria.
2. **Problem-seeking:** how to track customers owing me · debtors book ·
   how to keep records for my business · how to know if my business is
   making profit · separate business money from personal.
3. **Integrate-adjacent:** WhatsApp Business catalogue setup · how to
   receive payments on WhatsApp · Paystack payment link WhatsApp.
4. **Compliance (authority builders, research already done):** VAT small
   business Nigeria · e-invoicing mandate 2027 · business record keeping
   requirements Nigeria · CAC registration invoicing.

## Launch library — 12–16 guides, written during M1–M2 (2/week)

Each guide: genuinely useful standalone → schema.org `Article` + FAQ →
ends with a wa.me CTA. Priority order:

1. How to send a professional invoice on WhatsApp (2026)
2. Invoice requirements in Nigeria — what must appear on a valid invoice
3. VAT for small businesses: when you must and must not charge it
4. A debtors book that runs itself — tracking who owes you
5. How to set up a WhatsApp Business catalogue (step-by-step)
6. Receipt vs invoice: which to issue and when
7. What the 2027 e-invoicing mandate means for small businesses
8. How to keep business records the tax office will accept
9. How to receive payments on WhatsApp (transfer, Paystack, POS)
10. Pricing your products: knowing your real profit
11. Bookkeeping for market traders and physical shops
12. Handing your accountant clean records (and what they need monthly)

Then **programmatic vertical pages** once the pattern proves: "Bookkeeping
for fashion vendors / tailors / pharmacies / caterers / mechanics…" —
template + genuinely specific content per business type.

## Distribution (owned + earned, no paid at launch)

- Vendor communities: WhatsApp groups/status, Nairaland, X (NaijaTech),
  Instagram vendor niches.
- **Accountant channel — the wedge:** one accountant brings 10–30
  merchants; the accountant portal + Excel exports are the pitch. Build a
  simple partner page and referral tracking in M4.
- Built-in loop: free-tier documents carry a discreet "Created with Rekoda"
  credit (paid tiers clean) — the recipient of an invoice is exactly the
  next merchant.
- Launch sequencing: content warms during build → Chat launches publicly →
  Integrate alpha recruits from Chat's best users.

## Measurement

Plausible goals: guide → /start click · /start → OTP verified · OTP →
first WhatsApp message · first message → first document. One funnel, four
steps, reviewed weekly against which guide/page sourced the visit.
