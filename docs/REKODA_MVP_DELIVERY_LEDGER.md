## SUB-03r note (2 Nov 2026, autopilot run)

`sweepGracePeriods` claims a reminder day and re-reads eligibility in one transaction (a failed read
rolls the claim back) and afterwards releases it only when the send DEFINITELY did not leave
(`SendFailed.notSent`); a timeout or other unknown outcome keeps the claim and is logged as
unknown, so it is not resent (OWN-47; no exactly-once claim). A final eligibility check
(`subscriptionsRepo.graceReminderEligibility`: renewal still unpaid, plan still live, STOP/START read fresh)
runs immediately before dispatch. RED first: `does not resend a reminder whose outcome is unknown`
failed on `main` (a second notice went out). Also added: `suppresses a reminder when the cycle is
paid after the page was read`, `retries a reminder that definitely did not leave`. Local: billing
suite 344 tests at that point (347 at the final head), typecheck, lint, prettier green. MERGED (#400, `cefb6ee`), STAGING ACCEPTANCE PENDING.

Known residual (SUB-03r, accepted under OWN-47, not a defect): a reminder day is never retried after an
unknown outcome (a timeout, a Meta 5xx, or a process death between the claim commit and the send attempt), so
the day-5 reminder, the last one before the day-7 expiry, can be lost with no further warning. The same holds if a definite non-delivery is followed by a failed claim release: it is logged and the pass continues, and that day stays claimed. The
dashboard still shows the merchant's state. If the owner wants this softened (for example one bounded
retry for day 5, or treating clear gateway 5xx as not sent), that is a product decision, not an engineering fix.
