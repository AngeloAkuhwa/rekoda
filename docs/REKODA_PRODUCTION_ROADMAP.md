# Rekoda production roadmap

> 2 November 2026. Derived from the gap register, the launch backlog and the
> completion audit documents (`REKODA_PRODUCT_FEATURE_INVENTORY.md`,
> `REKODA_E2E_ACCEPTANCE_MATRIX.md`, `REKODA_SUBSCRIPTION_COMPLETION_AUDIT.md`).
> It invents no owner decision. **Estimates are engineering judgement, not
> commitments:** they assume one engineer working with an AI pair at the pace the
> repository shows (a focused slice in one to three days including review), and
> they exclude external lead times, which no one on the team controls.

## Workstreams

- **Engineering** (code the team writes), **Test gaps** (automated tests missing),
  **Staging acceptance** (owner exercises staging), **Provider** (Meta, Paystack,
  Mono, Resend, Anthropic, OpenAI), **Infrastructure and operations**, **Owner,
  legal and tax** (decisions only the owner or professionals can make).

## Assessment 1: controlled private beta

**Possible after P0 (roughly 3 to 4 weeks of engineering and owner time, gated by Meta template approval).** A beta of a small, named group of merchants who accept an unfinished edge: the trial, the chat, the books and the dashboard work; plans are set by the operator through the single audited writer, and **no card payments are taken** (so §47 and the Paystack drill are not on the beta path, and the paid-checkout code stays dark). Conditions: a real host with working restore (P0-1, P0-2), the OTP template approved so merchants can sign in (P0-3), the legal facts filled (P0-4), staging acceptance of the merged queue (P0-6), the live AI evaluation passed (P0-7), a support routine for the API-only operator tools, and the private-beta go/no-go in `REKODA_LAUNCH_READINESS.md` §14: no P0 or P1 row FAILs other than the rows §14 lets carry, unless the owner records a written deferral of that row in §6. §14 lets G-03, G-04 and G-05 carry into private beta (test WABA, staging legal placeholders, test-mode Paystack); this roadmap is stricter for G-03 and G-04 because real merchants join the beta, so P0-3 (OTP template approved) and P0-4 (legal facts filled) must pass and only G-05 carries (no card payments are taken). If the owner prefers the §14 minimum for G-03 and G-04, P0-3 and P0-4 relax to it; that is an owner choice, recorded here as open. Rows that would need such a deferral for beta include no DB-level plan control (G-62, OD-37), key rotation not built, retention figures not enforced (G-14) and a single OTP channel (G-19). An OPEN decision's recommendation (OD-37, OD-38) is not a ruling; until the owner rules or the gaps close, beta does not open.

## Assessment 2: full public launch

**Not before P0 and P1 are done: realistically two to three months, dominated by external parties (Paystack, Meta App Review, tax advice).** Required in addition to the beta list: Paystack confirmation and a successful real-money drill with refund and dispute handling verified (P1-1, P1-2), the subscription lifecycle proven on staging and with a real provider (P1-3), the database control on plan state (P1-4), key rotation (P1-5), enforced retention and erasure plus complete audit rows (P1-6), sign-in resilience (P1-7), App Review (P1-8), tax and legal sign-off (P1-9), verified monitoring and on-call (P1-10), load testing (P1-11), and an explicit owner ruling on point-in-time recovery: WAL archiving built, or the nightly-dump-only risk accepted in writing for public launch (P0-2, OD-7, `docs/safety-review.md` G12). Production deployment needs the owner's explicit instruction at that point.

## Critical path

Owner-held external items start first because they are the long poles: Meta business verification, template submission and App Review; the written Paystack answers; tax advice; the production host decision. In parallel, engineering finishes the P0 items (backups enabled, annual sales configured, staging redeploy), the owner runs the batched staging acceptance, then the live AI evaluation. Beta opens when P0 is signed and the §14 private-beta conditions hold (every P1 FAIL closed or deferred in writing by the owner in §6). P1 engineering runs during the beta; public launch opens only when every P1 acceptance condition passes, including the lifecycle certification (P1-3); P1-1 and P1-9 are the critical-path blockers because outside parties control them, not the whole gate.
