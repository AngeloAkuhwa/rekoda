# Rekoda production roadmap

> 2 November 2026. Derived from the gap register, the launch backlog and the
> completion audit documents (`REKODA_PRODUCT_FEATURE_INVENTORY.md`,
> `REKODA_E2E_ACCEPTANCE_MATRIX.md`, `REKODA_SUBSCRIPTION_COMPLETION_AUDIT.md`).
> It invents no owner decision. **Estimates are engineering judgement, not
> commitments:** they assume one engineer working with an AI pair at the pace the
> repository shows (a focused slice in one to three days including review), and
> they exclude external lead times, which no one on the team controls.

## Workstreams

- **Engineering** (code the team writes), **Test gaps** (automated tests missing),
  **Staging acceptance** (owner exercises staging), **Provider** (Meta, Paystack,
  Mono, Resend, Anthropic, OpenAI), **Infrastructure and operations**, **Owner,
  legal and tax** (decisions only the owner or professionals can make).

## P2: after launch

Central Admin UI; supplier identity unification and supplier page (G-33); cart stock hold (G-34); apply a customer credit to invoices (G-52); CG4 delivery verification (G-29); NestJS 12 and a dependency-vulnerability gate (G-42, G-70); card-on-file or auto-debit renewal; add-ons sold end to end (OWN-41 excluded them); scheduled and bulk customer reminders; Mono live feeds.

## Assessment 1: controlled private beta

**Possible after P0 (roughly 3 to 4 weeks of engineering and owner time, gated by Meta template approval).** A beta of a small, named group of merchants who accept an unfinished edge: the trial, the chat, the books and the dashboard work; plans are set by the operator through the single audited writer, and **no card payments are taken** (so §47 and the Paystack drill are not on the beta path, and the paid-checkout code stays dark). Conditions: the private-beta go/no-go in `REKODA_LAUNCH_READINESS.md` §14 is the gate, and this roadmap never relaxes it. In short: every P0 code gap is CLOSED, with no deferral path; GATES 1–11 show no P0 or P1 FAIL other than the carried G-03, G-04 and G-05 rows and P1 rows whose deferral an owner ruling in §6 records; journeys J1–J13, J17, J19 and J20 PASS on staging; a restore drill from an off-box backup ran within the last 7 days (P0-1, P0-2); the alert set has fired once each; the owner has signed the AI evaluation numbers (P0-7); G-48 has no deferral path. On top of §14 the roadmap adds staging acceptance of the merged queue (P0-6) and a support routine for the API-only operator tools. P1 rows that would need a §6 deferral for beta include no DB-level plan control (G-62, OD-37), key rotation not built, retention figures not enforced (G-14) and a single OTP channel (G-19); an OPEN decision's recommendation (OD-37, OD-38) is not a ruling. G-03 and G-04 (open owner choice): §14 lets them carry into beta on a test WABA and staging legal placeholders. This roadmap recommends, because real merchants join the beta, that the legal facts are filled (P0-4) and that every WhatsApp template beta uses is approved, at least OTP so merchants can sign in; which of the billing, retention, document and trial templates beta uses is for the owner to decide, and any template not approved must be disabled for beta. P0-3 as a whole (all templates approved) closes only for public launch. G-05 carries (test-mode Paystack; no card payments are taken).

## Assessment 2: full public launch

**Not before P0 and P1 are done: realistically two to three months, dominated by external parties (Paystack, Meta App Review, tax advice).** Required in addition to the beta list: Paystack confirmation and a successful real-money drill with refund and dispute handling verified (P1-1, P1-2), the subscription lifecycle proven on staging and with a real provider (P1-3), the database control on plan state (P1-4), key rotation (P1-5), enforced retention and erasure plus complete audit rows (P1-6), sign-in resilience (P1-7), App Review (P1-8), tax and legal sign-off (P1-9), verified monitoring and on-call (P1-10), load testing (P1-11), and an explicit owner ruling on point-in-time recovery: WAL archiving built, or the nightly-dump-only risk accepted in writing for public launch (P0-2, OD-7, `docs/safety-review.md` G12). Production deployment needs the owner's explicit instruction at that point.

## Critical path

Owner-held external items start first because they are the long poles: Meta business verification, template submission and App Review; the written Paystack answers; tax advice; the production host decision. In parallel, engineering finishes the P0 items (backups enabled, annual sales configured, staging redeploy), the owner runs the batched staging acceptance, then the live AI evaluation. Beta opens when the §14 private-beta go/no-go holds and the roadmap additions above are met. P1 engineering runs during the beta; public launch opens only when every P1 acceptance condition passes, including the lifecycle certification (P1-3); P1-1 and P1-9 are the critical-path blockers because outside parties control them, not the whole gate.
